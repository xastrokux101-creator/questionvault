Sync between devices

Questions are saved locally by default. To sync between devices, create a free Supabase project, set up the SQL and storage bucket described in README, and enter your project's public URL and publishable/anon key here. Never enter a service-role or secret key.

Sign in to sync

Use the same email and password on both devices. Sign up first; email confirmation may be required.
